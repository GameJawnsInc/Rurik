**Behaviour in one paragraph.** A press with a click leg in flight ends the
leg: latch and leg record cleared, one `0x002C` at the modelled body position
(the only message that halts the client's current segment *where the body is* —
a `0x0028` would halt the sync copy at the leg's start and warp), then the tick

the swing when the body stops at `r + r + 56 = 80 u`. Any move command forgets
the attack target and ends the follow; the swing already in flight still obeys
§32's landing split.
