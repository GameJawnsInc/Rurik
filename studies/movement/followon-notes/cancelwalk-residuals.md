### 2.2 The binary: who can fire the arrival, and who can clear `+0x48`

**OBSERVED** (`codescan.py` over the pinned pristine build-38797 client):

- `--field 0x48 --in AgAgent --writes` reproduces the orchestrator's census
  **exactly**: four stores, `0x005FE531`, `0x005FEAD6`, `0x005FEB46` (the grant
  bake, which arms) and `0x006021E6` `mov dword [ebx+0x48], 0` (the only clear).
- `--xrefs 0x006020B0` returns **7 direct `call rel32` references and 0 data
  words**: `0x0060032E, 0x00600A91, 0x00601817, 0x00601899, 0x0060221E,
  0x006025A6, 0x00602B74`. **Every one is inside the AgAgent module range
  0x005FE0C3..0x00602CE2.** Nothing in the skill/cast machinery calls the
  arrival primitive directly.
- *Positive control on that negative:* the same `--xrefs` call finds
  `0x00602B74` — the SetPosition to arrival call the orchestrator had already
  established independently — so the search is not returning a vacuous list.
- *Blind spots respected (codescan's own footer):* `--xrefs` cannot see indirect
  calls through a register or vtable; `--field` cannot see a field reached
  through a **biased `this`** (the measured `PyCliParty` case), a constant held
  in a register, or an address built in more than one step. So "no cast-side
  caller" is bounded as **"nothing stores or directly branches to it"**, not
  "nothing reaches it". I did not need to close that gap, because the capture
  settles the question from the other side (2.3).

## 5. R6's CONVERGED-COPY PRECONDITION — the clause, written to be checkable

### 5.1 The state of the arm today — OBSERVED

`--stop-answer=ack` sends one bare `0x0028` in answer to **every** player c2s
`0x0047` stop report (`authsrv.py:15052`). F34's caveat is already annotated in
two places — the flag's comment block (`:1569`, "R6's warp exposure is NONZERO")
and the runtime banner (`:17732`) — but **there is no precondition anywhere**:
nothing in the arm, the protocol at 7.4, or the scorer says what must be true of
the sync copy before an ack may be scored. This section supplies it.

### 5.2 Why an operator-side rule alone cannot carry it

The ack fires **at the stop**, and the stop is precisely the instant the sync
copy is furthest behind: our zero-lead policy grants only on `0x003D`, stops
grant nothing (section 0), so at a `0x0047` the copy is still walking in toward
the last granted point — up to the `0x003D` chord behind, measured max
**524.45 u** (8.3f). F34 caught exactly that state and measured a **167.6 u**
warp. So the operator cannot make every ack converged; the protocol has to
*identify* the converged ones and exclude the rest, and the identification is
only possible from a movetap tape.

### 5.3 THE CLAUSE — proposed text for CANCELWALK 7.4's protocol

> **R6-P — the converged-copy precondition.** Every `0x0028` this arm sends is
> only *scorable* if it landed on a converged copy.
>
> **Condition.** At the wall instant of a scored `0x0028` send, both the movetap
> sample immediately before and the one immediately after it must show:
> 1. **`sep <= 8.0 u`** — the sync and async copies co-located. (8.0 u is not
>    invented: it is the top of the pin arm's own measured pin-vs-body band over
>    the six reckoned casts of 8.3d/8.3f — 8.10, 6.66, 8.19, 4.12, 6.50,
>    2.96 u — the tightest agreement this instrument has ever demonstrated
>    between a server-set point and the drawn body.)
> 2. **`target == [inf, inf]`**, or `|target - async_at| <= 8.0 u` — no
>    destination armed, or one armed where the body already is. Without this a
>    copy can be *momentarily* co-located while still walking through, which is
>    the F35 regime rather than convergence.
>
> **Instrument requirement.** `movetap.py` MUST run for the whole session and its
> tape MUST cover every scored ack. **A wire-only R6 is VOID by construction:**
> `sep` and `target` appear nowhere on the wire, and 7.4c already established
> that a wire-only criterion was about to score a warp as a walk. This is not a
> nicety; it is what makes the run legible at all.
>
> **Operator protocol** (the ack fires on *every* stop, so the job is to
> manufacture one converged stop per rep, not to avoid unconverged ones):
> 1. Walk the approach leg and stop. **This stop's ack is expected to be
>    unconverged. Mark it EXCLUDED. Do not score it.**
> 2. Stand still, hands off keyboard and mouse, **>= 2.5 s.** Rationale, measured
>    rather than chosen: the ~512 u `0x003D` chord caps staleness at ~524 u
>    (8.3f measured max 524.45 u) and the copy closes at 288 u/s, so 1.82 s is
>    the chord-bounded worst case; 2.5 s carries a 37% margin.
> 3. Produce the **scored** stop with a short tap: press and release a movement
>    key for well under one chord (< ~1 s of travel). Its `0x003D` re-aims the
>    copy from a converged start, so its `0x0047` cannot be more than that tap's
>    worth of lag behind.
> 4. Only then make the cancelled cast the rep is testing.
>
> **VOID rules.** A rep is VOID if any of:
> - **(a)** no movetap tape covers the scored ack;
> - **(b)** the scored ack fails condition 1 or condition 2 in either bracketing
>   sample;
> - **(c)** the two bracketing samples straddle a gap **> 0.20 s**. The poll runs
>   at a measured median **0.079-0.083 s**, p95 0.093 s, max 0.122 s across the
>   two R10 tapes, so a > 0.20 s straddle means a dropped sample and the
>   precondition is unevaluable — not "probably fine";
> - **(d)** the drawn body's inter-sample step exceeds **35 u** anywhere in
>   `[ack - 0.5 s, ack + 1.0 s]` (35 u is 8.3c's own across-halt bar). A step
>   above it means a warp happened, the treatment is confounded, and the rep
>   measures F34 rather than H6.
>
> The RUN is VOID for H6 if **fewer than 3 reps survive** — 7.4's existing
> readout floor, now applied to *surviving* reps rather than attempted ones.
>
> **Scorer clause.** The scorer must report, per ack: wall time, `sep` before and
> after, `target` before and after, and the maximum inter-sample body step in the
> window; and must report **the count of EXCLUDED (unconverged) acks alongside
> the scored ones.** An R6 report that does not carry the excluded count has not
> shown that its scored acks were the converged ones — only that some ack
> somewhere behaved.

### 5.4 The cheaper alternative, named so it stays a choice and not a drift

The precondition exists because the arm sends a **bare** `0x0028`. The shipped
cast-stop already solved the same problem the other way: **PIN-OR-NOTHING** — a
dead-reckoned `0x002C` at the body's true point first, so the `0x0028` lands on
co-located copies and cannot snap (8.3e). A `--stop-answer=pin-ack` arm built
that way would satisfy R6-P by construction and need no operator ritual.

**It must be a separate arm, not a silent change to `ack`.** R6's hypothesis
(H6, stop-closure prior state) is about reproducing *retail's* bare stop-ack —
and retail's is genuinely bare. **OBSERVED across the whole live corpus:** 299
s2c `0x0028` against only **13** s2c `0x002C`, and the 13 are spawn / teleport /
resurrect events (four of them place an agent at the same `(9250, -1200)` spawn
point followed by a speed row; the rest ride `0xF1`/`0xA2` resurrect-shaped
bursts or a plane change). **Not one of the 13 is followed within two messages
by a `0x0028`.** Retail has no pin-pair shape at all. Prefixing a `0x002C` is
therefore a real change of treatment, and an arm that does it is testing a
different thing and must carry its own licensing paragraph. **UNVERIFIED**
whether the pinned form would still answer H6; that is the question such an arm
would have to register.
