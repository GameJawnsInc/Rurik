*** CONSEQUENCE FOR MOVECODE-B3 (the thing the lane was asked to decide) ***
AN ENTRY-ONLY HOOK CANNOT SCORE THE ANSWER. At the entry, arg5/arg6 are
uninitialised caller memory. To capture the RESULT you must either
  (a) also break at the four ret sites 0x00709F0F / 0x00709F44 / 0x0070A0AD /
      0x0070A0D4 and re-read [saved arg5] and [saved arg6]; or
  (b) capture the arg5/arg6 POINTER VALUES at entry, stash them, and read the
      buffers at the matching return.
The entry hook IS sufficient to capture the QUERY (from, to, range, arg4) -- so
an entry-only tap can still replay every query through pathmap.py; it just cannot
read the client's own answer to compare against.
